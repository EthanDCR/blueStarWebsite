## Tell him before you hang up

1. **The map pack is the prize, not the website.** Searching "welding denton tx"
   returns three map results above everything else, chosen mostly by Google
   Business Profile signals — proximity, reviews, categories. The site supports
   that and ranks below it. Set the expectation now.
2. **Photograph every single job**, before and after. Phone camera is fine.
   This is his homework starting today.
3. **Reviews are the lever.** Ask every customer, every time. A short link
   texted the day the job finishes converts best.
4. **Timeline is realistic, not instant.** A new domain with no citations takes
   roughly 3–6 months to show up and 6–12 to compete. GBP can rank in weeks.
5. **The DBA name is a real fork.** Exact-keyword names like "Denton Welding Co."
   measurably out-rank brand names like "Blue Star Welding Co." for this query.
   "Blue Star" is the better brand and GBP will suspend a name that doesn't match
   his signage — so this is his call, but it closes once he files.

## Reference: directory listings (citations)

A citation is his name/address/phone on a *third-party* site — separate from
GBP. Google crawls them as corroboration that the business is real, and uses
them as a tiebreaker in the pack.

| Listing | Why it's worth the time |
| --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | The map pack itself. Everything else is support. |
| Bing Places | Free, ~10 min, feeds Bing and AI search results |
| Apple Business Connect | Apple Maps + Siri — many iPhone users never open Google Maps |
| Facebook Business Page | Ranch customers genuinely find welders here |
| Nextdoor Business | Punches above its weight for local trades |
| Denton Chamber of Commerce | Citation *and* a real backlink from a local .org |
| Yelp | Low job volume for welding, but Google reads it |

**The rule:** identical NAP on every one. Not "Blue Star Welding Co" on one and
"Blue Star Welding Company LLC" on another. Not `(940) 555-0142` here and
`940-555-0142` there.

## Target queries

What we're building toward, roughly in order of value:

- `welding denton tx` — primary, highest competition
- `mobile welding denton` / `mobile welder near me`
- `ranch fence builder denton tx` / `pipe fence installation denton county`
- `cattle guard installation denton tx`
- `trailer repair welding denton`
- `welding sanger tx` / `krum` / `argyle` / `pilot point` — town pages, later

Town pages come **after** Denton ranks. A dozen near-identical location pages on
a brand-new domain is the textbook doorway-page pattern and risks a manual action.
